1 — basic view() with translate

The figure on the right drifts from translate: 0 30px to translate: 0 -30px across its scroll-through window. No will-change. No JS. Stop scrolling — it freezes. Scroll back — it reverses.

2 — bigger displacement (speed: 6)

Same recipe, magnitude doubled: 60px to -60px. The card moves further across the same viewport pass.

3 — opacity over view()

animation-timeline isn't limited to transforms. Here opacity ramps 0 → 1 → 0 over the scroll-through. Still composited.

4 — combined: translate + scale

Two individual transform properties animating in parallel: translate and scale. Both stay on the compositor fast-path.

5 — long block, the same engine

A taller card to confirm the math: full scroll window is viewport height + element height. The animation plays linearly across that window.

End of demo. Scroll back up to see the animations reverse.
